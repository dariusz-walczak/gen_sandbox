# Digitized Book Page {#digitized_book_page}

A __Digitized Book Page__ is a digital representation of one or more __[Book Pages](#book_page)__ that is a part of a __[Digitized Book](#digitized_book)__.

## Open Issues

- spread-scan-representation: a single scan or photograph often captures an open book and therefore represents two __[Book Pages](#book_page)__ at once (e.g., the __[Verso](#verso)__ of one __[Book Leaf](#book_leaf)__ and the __[Recto](#recto)__ of the next one); the definition above permits this ("one or more"), but the term name suggests a single page; consider whether a dedicated sibling term (e.g., Digitized Book Opening) would be clearer.
- multiple-representations: a single __[Book Page](#book_page)__ may have multiple digital representations (e.g., re-scans of an initially unreadable page); confirm that each such image is a distinct __Digitized Book Page__, which the current wording allows.
