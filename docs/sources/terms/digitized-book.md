# Digitized Book {#digitized_book}

A __Digitized Book__ is a digital representation of a __[Book](#book)__ consisting of one or more __[Digitized Book Pages](#digitized_book_page)__, created to improve the availability of the original material and to preserve it.

## Subterms

- \subpage digitized_book_page

## Notes

- The __[Digitized Book Pages](#digitized_book_page)__ of a __Digitized Book__ form a sequence (e.g., the order of scans or photographs) that usually mirrors the __[Reading Order](#reading_order)__ of the represented __[Book](#book)__, but may deviate from it (e.g., missing, duplicated, or out-of-order images). This sequence is a property of the __Digitized Book__ and is distinct from the __[Reading Order](#reading_order)__ itself.
