# Registrar {#registrar}

A __Registrar__ is a person responsible for maintaining official records, such as __[Registers](#register)__, or other legal or administrative documents. The __Registrar__ ensures that the records are accurately recorded and stored. They may also verify the authenticity of the information provided and issue official documents like birth certificates. A Registrar may be a __[Participant](#participant)__ of the __[Event](#event)__ being documented.
