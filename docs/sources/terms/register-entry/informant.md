# Informant {#informant}

An __Informant__ is the person who provides the information about a registered __[Event](#event)__ to the __[Registrar](#registrar)__ (e.g., the father for a birth, a neighbor or official for a death). An Informant may be a __[Participant](#participant)__ of the registered __Event__.
