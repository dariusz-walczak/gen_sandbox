# Witness {#witness}

A __Witness__ is a person who attests to the occurrence of an [Event](#event), such as [Birth Event](#birth_event), __Marriage Event__, or __Death Event__, by signing the official record or being present during the __Event__, thereby validating its accuracy and authenticity. A Witness is typically a [Secondary Participant](#secondary_participant) of the documented __Event__. Witnesses often include family members, neighbors, or local officials.
