# Knowledge Source: Terms and Definitions

## Archive {#archive}

An __Archive__ is a repository of records of historical, legal, or cultural significance. It also refers to the institution that preserves and provides access to those documents.

### Online Repository {#online_repository}

An __Online Repository__ is a repository of digital or digitized documents maintained by an __Archive__ or other organization.

## Administrative Scope {#administrative_scope}

An __Administrative Scope__ is the combination of geographical area and institutional or organizational boundaries (such as parishes, municipalities, or faith communities) within which __Registers__ or other official records are maintained. It may also include other historical or administrative differentiations (e.g., separate registers for different faiths, social classes, or legal jurisdictions) that define the context of the records.

## Birth Event {#birth_event}

A __Birth Event__ is a [Single Birth Event](#single_birth_event) or a [Multiple Birth Event](#multiple_birth_event).

### Multiple Birth Event {#multiple_birth_event}

A __Multiple Birth Event__ is a birth of multiplets.

### Single Birth Event {#single_birth_event}

A __Single Birth Event__ is a birth of an individual.

## Book

A __Book__ is a physical or digital volume consisting of multiple __Book Leaves__ bound together, used for recording, preserving, or presenting information.

### Book Leaf {#book_leaf}

A __Book Leaf__ is a single physical sheet of paper within a __Book__, containing two pages (__Recto__ and __Verso__).

### Book Page {#book_page}

A __Book Page__ is one side of a __Book Leaf__.

### Recto {#recto}

A __Recto__ is the __Book Page__ that appears first when progressing through the __Book__ from beginning to end.

### Verso {#verso}

A __Verso__ is the __Book Page__ that appears second when progressing through the __Book__ from beginning to end.

## Register {#register}

A __Register__ is a collection of __Register Entries__ of the same type (such as birth, death, or marriage entries), typically covering a continuous period within a specific __Administrative Scope__.

### Birth Register {#birth_register}

A __Birth Register__ is a __Register__ dedicated to recording and preserving information about births, including the names, dates, and places of birth of individuals, as well as details about their parents and other persons such as __Informants__ and witnesses.

### Death Register {#death_register}

A __Death Register__ is a __Register__ dedicated to recording and preserving information about deaths, including the names, dates, and places of death, as well as details about the deceased, their family members, and other persons such as __Informants__.

### Marriage Register {#marriage_register}

A __Marriage Register__ is a __Register__ dedicated to recording and preserving information about marriages, including the names of the individuals, dates and places of marriage, and details about their family members and other persons such as witnesses.

## Register Book {#register_book}

A __Register Book__ is a physical or digital volume containing one or more __Registers__, each of a distinct type, covering the same period and __Administrative Scope__.

### Digitized Register Book {#digitized_register_book}

A __Digitized Register Book__ is a physical __Register Book__ that was digitized to improve its availability and preserve the original material.

### General Register Book {#general_register_book}

A __General Register Book__ is a __Register Book__ containing __Registers__ of at least two different types, typically combining the __Birth Register__, __Death Register__, and __Marriage Register__.

### Register Book Supplementary Content {#register_book_supplementary_content}

A __Register Book Supplementary Content__ are parts of a __Register Book__ that are not the __Register Entries__, e.g. introductions, instructions, registry closings, and annotations.

## Register Entry {#register_entry}

A __Register Entry__ is an individual record within a __Register__.

### Register Entry Annotation {#register_entry_annotation}

A __Register Entry Annotation__ is a supplement to a __Registry Entry__, most often in a form of a marginal note, that typically corrects errors in the entry or provides information about other events associated with the primary participant.

#### Register Entry Correction Note {#register_entry_correction_note}

A __Register Entry Correction Note__ is a type of __Register Entry Annotations__ that corrects language or factual errors spotted in the __Register Entry__ by the __Registrar__, __Informant__, __Witnesses__, or other persons participating in the registration process. The correction is typically added immediatelly after the __Register Entry__ creation and due to this fast is typically undated.

### Birth Entry {#birth_entry}

A __Birth Entry__ is a __Register Entry__ recording a __Birth Event__.

### Informat {#informat}

An __Informant__ is the person person who provides the information about registered event to the __Registrar__ (e.g., the father for a birth, a neighbor or official for a death).

### Registrar {#registrar}

A __Registrar__ is a person responsible for maintaining official records, such as [Registers](#register), or other legal or administrative documents. The __Registrar__ ensures that the records are accurately recorded, and stored. They may also verify the authenticity of the information provided and issue official documents like birth certificates.

### Witness {#witness}

A __Witness__ is a person who attests to the occurrence of an event, such as [Birth Event](#birth_event), __Marriage Event__, or __Death Event__, by signing the official record or being present during the event, thereby validating its accuracy and authenticity. Witnesses often include family members, neighbors, or local officials.
